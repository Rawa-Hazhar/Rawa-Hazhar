### About me
I work where **physics, chemistry and artificial intelligence** meet.
- 🔬 Developing **AI-integrated simulation models for polymer materials** with the Advanced Polymeric Materials Research Lab, University of Sulaimani
- 🎓 Completing an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence**: machine learning, explainable AI (LIME, SHAP, SMILE) and fair AI
- 🧑‍🏫 Ten years teaching **physics and chemistry** in UK colleges and schools and in the Kurdistan Region (PGCE, BSc Chemistry)
- 🗣️ Building **AI tools for Kurdish (Sorani)**, a low-resource language: speech-to-text, learning apps and AI courses
- 🛡️ Cybersecurity and digital-safety training for organisations
